The ATS-friendly resume format

These are the format rules shared by the major ATS parsers. Follow them and your file reads cleanly almost everywhere. When you are done, run it through the free checker to confirm.

File

Use PDF or DOCX — whichever the posting asks for; PDF when it does not say. Never send a scanned image, a JPG/PNG of your resume, or a file exported flat from a design tool. Keep it under 2 MB and name it plainly: Firstname-Lastname-Resume.pdf.

Layout

Single column. No sidebars, no two-column templates, no text boxes, no tables. Read order on the page must be the order you want the parser to file things: contact, summary, experience (newest first), education, skills.

Headings

Use the standard words: Work Experience (or Experience), Education, Skills. Creative headings — "My Journey", "Where I've Been", "Superpowers" — are not recognised, and everything under them can be filed as nothing.

Contact details

Put your name, email, phone (with country code) and LinkedIn URL in the main body of page one — not in the page header or footer, which several parsers skip entirely.

Fonts and characters

Standard fonts (Arial, Calibri, Georgia, Helvetica, Times) at 10–12 pt. Use simple round bullet characters. Avoid icons, skill bars, star ratings, logos and photos — the parser cannot read any of them, and a "90% Photoshop" bar becomes empty space.

Dates

Give every job and qualification a month and year (e.g. Mar 2021 – Jun 2024). The ATS calculates your total experience from these dates; missing dates can zero it out.

Length

One page under ~5 years of experience, two pages above that. Detail the last 10 years; compress older roles to a line each.

The 10-second self-test

Open your PDF, select all (Ctrl/Cmd + A), copy, and paste into a plain text editor. If the pasted text reads in the right order with nothing missing, an ATS can read it too. If it comes out scrambled, so will your application.
