Why resumes get rejected before a human sees them

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) are databases with a strict reader attached. If the reader cannot pull clean text out of your file — because it is a scanned image, a two-column design, or built with tables — your profile is created empty or scrambled, and keyword searches by recruiters never find you. It is rarely your experience that fails. It is the file.

Honest about what a checker can promise

Every company's ATS is configured differently, and none of them publish their scoring. Any tool that promises you an exact "Workday score" or a guaranteed interview is guessing. ResumeStow gives you an ATS readiness estimate based on the parsing rules these systems share — readable text, single column, standard headings, and the job's own keywords used honestly. That is the part you can control, and it is the part that silently filters out qualified people every day.
