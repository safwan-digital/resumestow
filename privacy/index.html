Privacy policy

Your resume never leaves your device. ResumeStow reads and analyses your file entirely inside your browser. No resume, job description, name, email or score is uploaded to any server, stored, or shared with anyone. When you close the tab, everything is gone. There are no accounts and no sign-up.

What we keep on your device

One small preference — light or dark theme — saved in your browser's local storage. Nothing else.

Analytics

ResumeStow launched without third-party analytics. If privacy-friendly, aggregate visit counting is added later, this page will be updated first, and it will never include file content or report data — those never leave your device in the first place.
