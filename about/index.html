About ResumeStow

ResumeStow exists because of a simple, unfair problem: qualified people are filtered out of jobs by software that could not read their resume file — a two-column template, a scanned PDF, the wrong heading word — and nobody ever tells them. The rejection email says nothing. There is nothing to fix, because nothing was ever explained.

Other checkers answer this with a signup wall, a server upload of your most personal document, and a paywall in front of the fixes. ResumeStow does the opposite: free, no sign-up, and your file is analysed inside your own browser and never uploaded.

How the score works

The ATS Readiness Score (0–100) combines four parts: format and parseability (30) — can the file be read at all; sections and contact details (15); content quality (15) — action verbs, numbers, dates; and, when you paste a job description, keyword matching (40) against the words that job repeats most. It is an honest estimate based on the parsing rules the major ATS platforms share — each company's system differs, and we say so on the report itself.
